HOW TO PLAY RISHU KEY JUTSU

🥋 Type to Strike: Every correct letter typed instantly triggers a martial arts punch, kick, or uppercut using trailer hit sounds.

⚡ Combos & Finishers: Typing strings of letters without mistakes builds combo multipliers. Completing a full word executes a heavy KO finisher strike.

⚠️ Beware Typos: Typos stagger your ninja and give enemies an opening to counter-attack and reduce your health.

🗺️ Progressive Arenas: Battle across 100 levels spanning 11 unique arenas, from the Training Dojo to the Final Key Summit.
