Privacy Policy

LAST UPDATED: SEPTEMBER 02, 2026 Rishu Key Jutsu 100% Offline Game

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy describes the privacy practices for Rishu Key Jutsu, developed by Rishu Key Jutsu (operating under Rishu Key Jutsu). It covers both the standalone Rishu Key Jutsu game application and the official website.

We believe in complete transparency. Rishu Key Jutsu is designed as a privacy-respecting, single-player martial arts touch-typing game. Rishu Key Jutsu does not collect, track, aggregate, or sell your personal data.

2. Rishu Key Jutsu Standalone Game (100% Offline)

In Short: The Rishu Key Jutsu game software is 100% offline. It collects zero personal data, contains zero tracking, and initiates zero external network connections.

When you play Rishu Key Jutsu on your desktop device:

  • No Network Connections: The game software does not connect to the internet and contains zero background HTTP requests or network sockets.
  • No Tracking: We do not include any tracking SDKs (such as Google Analytics or Unity Analytics) or crash reporting services.
  • No In-Game Ads: The game contains no advertisements, behavioral profiling tools, or ad networks.
  • No Account Required: You do not need to register an account, log in, or provide an email address to play Rishu Key Jutsu.

3. Local Save Data

All gameplay progress, star ratings, typing speed (WPM) scores, accuracy metrics, and audio preferences are stored exclusively on your local device.

• Unlocked Levels & Stars: user://keyjutsu_save.json
• Typing Profile & WPM Metrics: user://keyjutsu_typing_profile.json
• Audio Settings & Volume: user://keyjutsu_audio_settings.json

These local save files remain under your complete control. They are never uploaded, synced, or transmitted to any external server. You can reset or delete this data at any time through the in-game options menu or by deleting the local save directory on your computer.

4. Official Website & Host Server Logs

When you visit the official Rishu Key Jutsu website:

  • No Advertising Pixels or Tracking Cookies: The website does not use tracking cookies, Google Analytics, Meta Pixels, or cross-site tracking scripts.
  • Host Server Logs: Standard web hosting infrastructure automatically logs basic technical request data (such as your IP address, browser type, and timestamp) solely for network security, DDoS protection, and operational server delivery. Rishu Key Jutsu does not store or process these host logs for tracking or profiling.

5. Support & Email Communications

If you voluntarily contact us via email at ysrishabh017@gmail.com for customer support or feedback, we receive your email address and the message contents you provide. We use this information solely to respond to your inquiry. We do not sell, rent, or share your contact information with third parties.

6. Storefront Platforms & Billing

Rishu Key Jutsu may be purchased or downloaded through official storefront platforms such as Steam or itch.io. Purchases and payments are processed directly by the respective storefront platform under their own terms and privacy policies. Rishu Key Jutsu does not collect or process your credit card, bank, or billing information.

7. Children's Privacy

Because Rishu Key Jutsu is a 100% offline standalone application and does not collect personal information or require user registration, children can use the software without submitting personal data.

8. Your Privacy Rights

Under applicable data protection laws (such as GDPR, CCPA, and PIPEDA), you have rights regarding your personal information. Because Rishu Key Jutsu does not collect personal data during gameplay, your local game data remains under your direct control on your device.

For any support email communications or formal privacy inquiries, you can contact us directly by emailing ysrishabh017@gmail.com.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated "LAST UPDATED" date.
